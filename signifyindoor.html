Vertical Epicyclic Gearboxes – Cooling-Water Pumps

Our vertical epicyclic geared pump drive enables significantly smaller electric motors compared to traditional direct-coupled motor drives. This size reduction contributes to substantial cost savings in installation and operation of a typical circulating pump package.

Key Design Features
  • Gear units designed to suit motor and pump interfaces
  • Gearcase can be designed to support the weight of the drive motor
  • Reduced overall package size
  • Coaxial drive train (motor, gearbox, pump)
  • 99% mechanical efficiency
  • Self-contained full-duty lubrication system
  • Reduced noise and vibration
  • Transfers thrust or accepts external thrust via integral thrust bearing
  • Eliminates the need for a low-speed coupling
Efficiency 99% mechanical efficiency

Hydro-Turbine Gearboxes – Power Generation

As a leading supplier to the power-generation industry, we have extensive experience in applying technology to hydro-turbine projects. Our geared system enables driving a lower-cost, high-speed generator with a low-speed turbine, improving efficiency.

Key Features
  • High efficiency of typically 99%, directly translating into additional revenue
  • Co-axial arrangement reduces overall size
  • Compact, smaller, and lighter than alternatives, reducing overall system size
  • Lower pitch-line velocity reduces noise
  • Horizontal and vertical arrangements available
  • Multiple stage solutions for high-ratio speed increasers
Power Output 1–30 MW for low-head turbines running at 40–200 rpm
Technology Allen Gears epicyclic technology, known for superior efficiency and reliability
