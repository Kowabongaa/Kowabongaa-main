Lufkin Gears

Our Lufkin Gears and Allen Gears brands bring a deep history of industry-leading technology to demanding high-speed mechanical-drive, turbo machinery, and marine-propulsion applications.

Allen Gears

Lufkin Gears low-speed, increasing, reduction, and parallel shaft gears are widely used with turbines, pumps, electric motors, generators, and compressors in many heavy industries and are renowned for reliable performance in the most demanding applications.

Specifications

Overview Lufkin Gears and Allen Gears offer industry-leading technology for high-speed mechanical-drive, turbo machinery, and marine-propulsion applications. Designs include single and double helical gearing and fluid-film bearing technology for high performance and reliability.
Key Features
  • Purpose-built to exacting customer specifications and ISO/AGMA/API standards
  • Robust designs with rigid case construction
  • Speeds up to 70,000 rpm and power up to 100 MW
  • Low noise, low vibration, and industry-leading material specifications
  • Extended maintenance intervals
  • Cast and fabricated casings
  • Low overall cost of ownership with exceptional reliability and long life
  • Integrated lubrication, vibration, and temperature monitoring
Compressors Lufkin Gears drive compressors in offshore platforms, refineries, and petrochemical plants with motors, engines, and turbines at up to 90 MW and speeds of 40,000 rpm, ensuring reliability for critical operations.
Pumps Used in critical pump applications worldwide, Lufkin Gears drive axial, centrifugal, and screw pumps with power up to 40 MW and speeds of 25,000 rpm, providing decades of dependable service.
Generators In power generation, Lufkin Gears connect steam and gas turbines up to 90 MW to generators. Integrated clutch and brake systems ensure flexibility and maximum uptime for plant operations.
Fans & Blowers For applications up to 12 MW, Lufkin Gears drive fans and blowers in industries such as petrochemical, chemical, and power plants, offering high efficiency and customer-specific designs.

Category Description
Overview Lufkin Gears low-speed, increasing, reduction, and parallel shaft gears are used with turbines, pumps, electric motors, generators, and compressors in heavy industries. They are known for reliable performance in demanding applications.
Rubber and Plastics Lufkin Gears speed reducers are used in tire production for batch mixers, mills, extruders, calendars, and roll mills. Known for their reliability, they offer excellent value for rubber and plastics industries.
Sugar Lufkin Gears has provided multi-stage speed reducers for the sugar industry for over 50 years. Many mill drives and cane knives are still operational after decades. Custom solutions include parallel shaft and planetary gears for mills and cane diffusers.
Mining and Aggregate Lufkin Gears technologies support crushers, pumps, slew drives, bucket wheels, conveyor drives, and more in harsh mining environments. Available in various configurations such as horizontal or vertical parallel shaft, angular shaft, and planetary designs.
Hydro Power Known for reliability in hydro-electric plants, Lufkin Gears parallel-shaft increasers and Allen Gears epicyclic products are widely used in turbine applications, powering some of the world’s largest hydro plants.
